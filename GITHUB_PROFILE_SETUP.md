## What not to add back

Avoid profile-view counters, GitHub trophies, typing animations, generic skill walls, “A+ developer” stats cards, random quotes and marketing descriptions such as `revenue-ready`, `scalable platform`, `flagship Product Engineer` or `quantified product impact`.

The profile works better when the evidence is the projects themselves.
